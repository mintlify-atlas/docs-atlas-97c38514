Coffee Tycoon is an idle incremental game where a single click on a coffee cup sets off a chain reaction — earning coffee you spend on buildings that earn more coffee while you're away. The loop is simple to start and surprisingly deep to master.

## The core game loop

Every run follows the same cycle:

1. **Click** the coffee cup in the Brew House to earn coffee manually.
2. **Spend** that coffee in the Shop on buildings that generate coffee automatically.
3. **Unlock** upgrades that double or multiply what your buildings produce.
4. **Prestige** when you hit 10 billion coffee to earn Golden Coffee and a permanent production multiplier, then start the loop again — faster.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start clicking">
    Open the **Brew House** tab (key `1`). Click the ☕ button to earn 1 coffee per click. This is your only income source until you buy your first building.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy your first building">
    Switch to the **Shop** tab (key `2`). When you have 50 coffee, buy your first **Auto Brewer** — it generates 5 coffee per second (CPS) automatically, so you earn coffee without clicking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy upgrades">
    Open the **Upgrades** tab (key `3`). Upgrades unlock when you meet their conditions (for example, owning 10 of a building). Each upgrade doubles the production of a specific building or provides a global multiplier. These are permanent until you prestige.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prestige for Golden Coffee">
    Once you've brewed 10 billion coffee total, the **Prestige** tab (key `4`) lets you reset your run in exchange for **Golden Coffee**. Each point of Golden Coffee adds a permanent +10% production multiplier that carries across all future runs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The five tabs

The game is organized into five tabs. Switch between them by clicking the tab buttons or pressing the corresponding number key.

| Tab | Key | What it does |
|-----|-----|--------------|
| Brew House | `1` | Manual brewing — click the ☕ button to earn coffee |
| Shop | `2` | Buy and sell buildings that automate coffee production |
| Upgrades | `3` | Purchase Regular and Golden Upgrades that multiply output |
| Prestige | `4` | Reset your run for Golden Coffee and a permanent multiplier |
| Achievements | `5` | Track milestones and claim coffee or multiplier rewards |

<Tip>
  Keyboard shortcuts are enabled by default. You can toggle them off in **Settings** (the ⚙️ button in the top-right corner) if you prefer to use the mouse only.
</Tip>

### Brew House (key 1)

The Brew House is where every run starts. Click the ☕ button to earn coffee manually. Your base click power starts at 1 coffee per click. Click power upgrades (unlocked in the **Upgrades** tab after earning enough total coffee) raise this: **Better Coffee Beans** adds +1 per click, **Premium Coffee Beans** adds +2, and **Exotic Coffee Beans** adds +5.

### Shop (key 2)

The Shop lists all 16 buildings, from the **Auto Brewer** (50 coffee, 5 CPS) up to the **Coffee Multiverse** (100 quadrillion coffee, 10 quadrillion CPS). Buildings you haven't unlocked yet appear once you have at least 50% of their base cost. You can buy and sell buildings in quantities of ×1, ×10, or ×100 using the mode buttons at the top of the tab.

### Upgrades (key 3)

The Upgrades tab has two sections:

- **Regular Upgrades** — unlock when you own enough of a specific building (for example, 10 Auto Brewers unlocks "Upgraded Auto Brewer", which doubles Auto Brewer production). Each building has 8 upgrade tiers. There are also global upgrades that multiply all buildings at once.
- **Golden Upgrades** — purchased with Golden Coffee earned through prestige. These include automation features (auto-buy buildings, auto-buy upgrades, auto-claim achievements) and permanent CPS bonuses (+5%, +10%, +20%) that survive resets.

### Prestige (key 4)

Prestige resets your coffee count, all buildings, and all regular upgrades. You keep your Golden Coffee, your achievements, and any permanent CPS bonuses from Golden Upgrades. The Golden Coffee you hold sets your prestige multiplier: each point adds 10%, so 5 Golden Coffee gives a 1.5× multiplier applied to all CPS.

### Achievements (key 5)

Achievements are grouped into packs — Coffee Milestones, CPS Milestones, General Collection, Upgrade Collection, and per-building packs. Earning an achievement gives you a reward (coffee or a production multiplier) that you claim manually, or automatically if you've purchased the **Auto-Claim Achievements** Golden Upgrade.

## The status bar

The header shows three stats that update continuously:

- **Coffee** — your current coffee balance, used to buy buildings and upgrades.
- **CPS** — coffee per second, the total automatic income from all your buildings combined (after multipliers).
- **Golden Coffee** — your accumulated Golden Coffee balance from prestige, used to buy Golden Upgrades.

Numbers above 1,000 are abbreviated (1k, 1M, 1B, 1T, and so on, up to trigintillion). You can switch to full numbers in **Settings**.
