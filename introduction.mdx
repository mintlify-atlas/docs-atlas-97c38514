Coffee Tycoon is a free-to-play idle game playable directly in your browser at [coffeetycoon.github.io](https://coffeetycoon.github.io/). Start by clicking a coffee cup to brew manually, then build up an automated empire spanning Auto Brewers, Baristas, Coffee Factories, and eventually a Coffee Multiverse producing astronomical quantities per second.
