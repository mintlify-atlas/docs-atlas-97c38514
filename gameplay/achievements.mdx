The Achievements tab tracks every major milestone in your Coffee Tycoon run. Achievements unlock automatically as you hit thresholds — brewing enough coffee, owning enough buildings, reaching a CPS target — but their rewards only pay out when you actively claim them. Each reward is either an immediate coffee bonus or a permanent production multiplier for a specific building type, making the Achievements tab worth checking regularly, especially after a prestige.

## How achievements work

Achievements are organized into **packs** (collapsible groups). Within each pack, only earned achievements and the next in-progress one are visible at a time — the rest are hidden until you're close enough to unlock them. Packs can be collapsed to save space; your collapsed state is saved automatically.

When an achievement unlocks while you're on another tab, a notification appears. If you're already on the Achievements tab when one unlocks, it becomes available to claim immediately with no notification.

### Claiming rewards

<Steps>
  <Step title="Earn the achievement">
    Meet the condition (e.g., brew 1,000 coffee, own 50 total buildings). The achievement is marked as earned and queued as unclaimed. A notification appears if you are on another tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Achievements tab">
    Navigate to the **Achievements** tab. Packs with unclaimed rewards show a numbered badge in the top corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the achievement pack">
    Click any achievement pack to open its detail modal, which shows all visible achievements in that pack with their progress bars and rewards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click an unclaimed achievement to claim it">
    Unclaimed achievements appear highlighted. Click directly on the achievement card in the modal to collect its reward. Coffee rewards are added to your current balance immediately. Multiplier rewards apply to that building's production permanently (surviving prestige).
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **Auto-Claim Achievements** Golden Upgrade automates step 2 and 3 — rewards are collected the instant they unlock, without any manual action required.

## Tips for managing achievements

- **Claim building multiplier rewards before prestiging.** These bonuses persist through resets and apply immediately in your next run.
- **Let Auto-Claim Achievements handle the rest.** Coffee rewards and general pack rewards are easily automated; reserve your attention for the multiplier rewards if you want to see the exact bonus text.
- **Collapse packs you've completed.** The game saves your collapsed pack state, so you can keep finished packs out of the way while focusing on in-progress ones.
- **CPS milestone achievements re-unlock each run.** Because your CPS rebuilds from zero after prestige, you'll collect those coffee rewards again — faster each time as your prestige multiplier grows.
