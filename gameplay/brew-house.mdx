The Brew House is where your coffee empire begins. Every run starts here — a single click on the coffee cup earns you coffee, and stacking Click Power upgrades turns each tap into a progressively more powerful production burst. Even as automation takes over, click power scales into the mid and late game as a meaningful supplement to your CPS.

## How Manual Clicking Works

Clicking the coffee cup button on the Brew House tab adds coffee equal to your current **click power**, multiplied by your **prestige multiplier**. At the start of a fresh run, click power is `1`, so each click earns exactly 1 coffee (or more if you have a prestige multiplier active).

The formula behind every click:

```
coffee earned = clickPower × prestigeMultiplier
```

Your current click power is always displayed beneath the coffee button: **Each click: +N Coffee**.

## Click Power Upgrades

Three upgrades in the Upgrades tab permanently increase your click power. Each is a one-time purchase that unlocks once you have brewed enough total coffee all-time.

| Upgrade | Cost | Click Power Bonus | Unlocks After |
|---|---|---|---|
| Better Coffee Beans | 1,500 coffee | +1 per click | 1,000 total coffee brewed |
| Premium Coffee Beans | 15,000 coffee | +2 per click | 10,000 total coffee brewed |
| Exotic Coffee Beans | 150,000 coffee | +5 per click | 100,000 total coffee brewed |

After purchasing all three, your base click power reaches **9** before any prestige multiplier is applied. With a 2× prestige multiplier, every click earns 18 coffee — a solid supplement to automated production even at mid-game building counts.

<Note>
  Click Power upgrades use your total all-time coffee brewed as their unlock condition, not your current coffee balance. You never lose progress toward them by spending coffee.
</Note>

## How Click Power Scales Through the Game

- **Early game (0–1k coffee):** Click power starts at 1. Focus on clicking to reach the 1,000 all-time threshold so Better Coffee Beans unlocks.
- **Mid early game (1k–15k coffee):** Purchase Better Coffee Beans (+1) for 1,500 coffee. Keep clicking and spending on Auto Brewers.
- **Late early game (15k–150k coffee):** Premium Coffee Beans (+2) becomes available. By now your Auto Brewers provide background CPS, but click bursts still contribute.
- **Mid game (150k+ coffee):** Exotic Coffee Beans (+5) brings click power to 9. Combined with prestige multipliers from future resets, each click can deliver dozens of coffee — useful for manually pushing toward upgrade cost thresholds.

After the three upgrades are purchased, click power only grows via the prestige multiplier. Each Golden Coffee earned through prestige adds 10% to your production multiplier, which applies to both CPS and manual clicks.

## Maximizing Click Power Early Game

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click to 1,000 total coffee">
    Open the Brew House and click the coffee cup until your all-time total reaches 1,000. This unlocks **Better Coffee Beans** in the Upgrades tab. You do not need to save the coffee — all-time total is never reset by spending.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy your first Auto Brewers">
    Head to the Shop and purchase a few Auto Brewers (50 coffee each) to begin passive CPS. This frees you to click less frantically while your total coffee count climbs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Purchase Better Coffee Beans">
    Once you have 1,500 coffee available, go to the Upgrades tab and buy **Better Coffee Beans**. Your click power jumps from 1 to 2 immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grind to 10,000 total coffee">
    Continue clicking and letting Auto Brewers accumulate coffee. When your all-time total reaches 10,000, **Premium Coffee Beans** unlocks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Purchase Premium Coffee Beans">
    Spend 15,000 coffee on **Premium Coffee Beans** to add +2 more per click. Click power is now 4 (or higher with prestige multipliers).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reach 100,000 total coffee for Exotic Coffee Beans">
    By this point you likely have several buildings running. Once all-time coffee hits 100,000, **Exotic Coffee Beans** unlocks for 150,000 coffee, adding +5 per click and capping the click power upgrade chain at a base of 9.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use the keyboard shortcut **1** to instantly switch to the Brew House tab, and **3** to switch to Upgrades, so you can quickly check for newly unlocked Click Power upgrades without leaving your clicking rhythm.
</Tip>
