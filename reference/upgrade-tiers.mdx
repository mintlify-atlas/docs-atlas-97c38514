Upgrades are the primary way to multiply your production without buying more buildings. Each of the 16 [buildings](/reference/buildings) has 8 tiers of upgrades, giving **128 building upgrades** in total. On top of those, there are 3 click power upgrades and 2 global production upgrades.

## Building upgrade tiers

All 8 tiers follow the same pattern for every building: each tier **doubles that building's CPS** (applies a ×2 multiplier to `itemMultipliers[id]`), unlocks once you own enough copies, and costs a fixed multiple of the building's base cost.

| Tier | Name | Buildings Required | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Upgraded | 10 | baseCost × 10 |
| 2 | Advanced | 25 | baseCost × 100 |
| 3 | Industrial | 50 | baseCost × 1,000 |
| 4 | Quantum | 100 | baseCost × 10,000 |
| 5 | Hyper | 200 | baseCost × 100,000 |
| 6 | Ultra | 400 | baseCost × 1,000,000 |
| 7 | Mega | 700 | baseCost × 10,000,000 |
| 8 | Divine | 1,000 | baseCost × 100,000,000 |

Multipliers stack multiplicatively. Purchasing all 8 tiers for a single building results in a **256× production multiplier** (2⁸) on top of any other bonuses.

**Example — Auto Brewer (baseCost 50):**

| Tier | Cost | Cumulative multiplier |
|---|---|---|
| Upgraded | 500 | ×2 |
| Advanced | 5,000 | ×4 |
| Industrial | 50,000 | ×8 |
| Quantum | 500,000 | ×16 |
| Hyper | 5,000,000 | ×32 |
| Ultra | 50,000,000 | ×64 |
| Mega | 500,000,000 | ×128 |
| Divine | 5,000,000,000 | ×256 |

<Tip>
Prioritize upgrade tiers for whichever building currently contributes the most to your total CPS. A single tier on your highest-output building typically yields a larger absolute CPS gain than all tiers on a lower-output one.
</Tip>

## Click power upgrades

Three upgrades increase the coffee earned per manual click. They unlock based on your **total all-time coffee brewed**, not your current balance.

| Upgrade | Effect | Cost | Unlock Condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Better Coffee Beans | +1 coffee per click | 1,500 | 1K total coffee |
| Premium Coffee Beans | +2 coffee per click | 15,000 | 10K total coffee |
| Exotic Coffee Beans | +5 coffee per click | 150,000 | 100K total coffee |

After all three are purchased, your base click power is **9** (1 base + 1 + 2 + 5).

## Global upgrades

Two upgrades apply a production multiplier to **every** building simultaneously. They unlock once all 16 buildings meet the ownership threshold.

| Upgrade | Effect | Cost | Unlock Condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Efficient Operations | All buildings ×1.5 | 500K | ≥10 of every building |
| Coffee Revolution | All buildings ×2 | 5M | ≥25 of every building |

Both upgrades apply their multiplier to each building's `itemMultipliers` entry, so they stack with individual tier upgrades multiplicatively. Owning both global upgrades alongside all 8 tiers for a building results in a combined building multiplier of **256 × 1.5 × 2 = 768×**.
