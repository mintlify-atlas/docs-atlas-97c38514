---
title: "How the Prestige System and Multiplier Work"
description: "Explains the exact formula Coffee Tycoon uses to award Golden Coffee on prestige and how that converts into a permanent production multiplier."
---

Prestiging resets your coffee, buildings, and upgrades in exchange for Golden Coffee — a permanent currency that raises a multiplier applied to all future production. The more total coffee you have brewed across your entire run, the more Golden Coffee you earn. This page documents the exact calculation used by `doPrestige()`.

## The formula

```js
goldenCoffeeToGain = min(100, floor(log2(totalCoffeeAllTime / 10,000,000,000 + 1)))
prestigeMultiplier = 1.0 + (goldenCoffee × 0.1)
```

The threshold base is **10 billion** (10,000,000,000) total coffee earned all-time. Each time your lifetime coffee production doubles past that threshold, you qualify for one additional Golden Coffee, up to a maximum of 100.

## Reference table

| Total Coffee Earned | Golden Coffee Total | Prestige Multiplier |
|---|---|---|
| 10 billion | 1 | 1.1× |
| 20 billion | 1 | 1.1× |
| 30 billion | 2 | 1.2× |
| 50 billion | 2 | 1.2× |
| 100 billion | 3 | 1.3× |
| 1 trillion | 6 | 1.6× |
| 10 trillion | 9 | 1.9× |
| ~10.23 trillion | 10 | 2.0× |
| 100 quadrillion | 23 | 3.3× |

"Golden Coffee Total" is the **total** you would hold after prestiging at that amount of lifetime coffee, not the amount earned in a single prestige. You only receive the difference between this total and your current Golden Coffee count.

## Incremental gains

You only receive the **difference** between your current Golden Coffee total and what you would earn at your current lifetime production. If you already hold 3 Golden Coffee and your next prestige qualifies you for 5, you gain **2** Golden Coffee — not 5.

This means the reward for each successive prestige gets smaller the longer you wait between them, but waiting also lets lifetime coffee grow further, eventually pushing you into a higher bracket.

## The 100 Golden Coffee cap

Golden Coffee is hard-capped at **100**. At that cap the prestige multiplier reaches `1.0 + (100 × 0.1) = 11.0×`. The cap is enforced both when prestiging and when importing a save. Once you reach 100 Golden Coffee, further prestiging provides no additional multiplier benefit.

## Golden Coffee never resets

Prestiging resets coffee on hand, all buildings, all regular upgrades, and click power. It does **not** reset Golden Coffee. Every Golden Coffee you have ever earned is permanent and continues to contribute to `prestigeMultiplier` from the very first second of a new run.

Golden upgrades purchased with Golden Coffee are also kept through prestige.

<Tip>
Prestige when you can gain at least 20–30% more multiplier than you currently hold. For example, if you are at `1.3×` (3 GC), wait until you qualify for at least 4 GC (`1.4×`) before resetting. Early in the game each new Golden Coffee is a large relative gain; later the jumps require exponentially more lifetime coffee, so patience pays more dividends.
</Tip>
