The roadmap below covers features currently planned for Coffee Tycoon. Each version builds on the last, adding new Golden Upgrade buildings, production mechanics, and player-facing tools.

<Info>
Coffee Tycoon is actively developed. New releases ship regularly, and planned features may be added, adjusted, or reordered as development progresses.
</Info>

<Note>
All roadmap items are subject to change. Features listed here reflect current plans and may be modified before release.
</Note>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="v1.14 – Grab a Lab">
    The Research Lab arrives as a new Golden Upgrade that unlocks a dedicated main tab.

    - **Research Lab:** A new tab unlocked as a Golden Upgrade, focused on discovering drink recipes.
    - **Drink recipes:** Discover approximately 10 unique recipes, each with a mix of buffs and debuffs to your production.
    - **Active drink limit:** Run a maximum of 3 drinks at a time. Swapping a drink out starts a regeneration timer before that slot is available again.
    - **Golden Upgrades for the building:** Additional permanent upgrades tied to the Research Lab itself.
    - **Offline CPS:** The game records the time you last played and calculates the coffee you would have earned while away, then awards it when you return.
    - **Live coffee display:** Your coffee total updates visually every 0.1 seconds to show the instant change from CPS, rather than updating on a delayed tick.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="v1.15 – Roasters and Coasters">
    The Roastery adds a full bean-processing system as another new Golden Upgrade main tab.

    - **Roastery:** A new tab unlocked as a Golden Upgrade for brewing blends from raw beans.
    - **Bean purchasing and processing:** Buy raw beans and process them into blends — Light, Medium, Dark, French, and more.
    - **Blend activation:** Activate a blend to apply a temporary global CPS boost.
    - **Golden Upgrades for the building:** Permanent upgrades specific to the Roastery.
    - **Bean cost scaling:** Each bean starts at 1 billion CPS cost and scales by ×1.15 per bean purchased across your lifetime.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="v1.16 – Super Coffee">
    A new layer of randomness and rewards with clickable bonuses that appear on-screen.

    - **Super Coffee buttons:** Randomly spawning clickable buttons that appear somewhere on the screen and grant temporary production bonuses when clicked.
    - **Golden Super Coffee:** Occasionally appears as a purchasable variant with stronger, longer-lasting buffs.
    - **Coffee storms:** Random events that temporarily multiply all CPS by 2× to 5× for a limited duration.
    - **Achievements:** New achievements tied to collecting Super Coffees.
    - **Mystery Coffee Beans:** Rare spawns that award unique or unpredictable boosts when collected.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="v1.17 – Stats Window">
    A dedicated stats panel giving you visibility into everything your empire has produced.

    - **Stats button:** Opens a panel with separate tabs for total coffee earned, current CPS, and Golden Coffee collected.
    - **Per-building graphs:** Individual CPS graphs for each building, plus a combined CPS view.
    - **Production timeline:** A historical chart of your coffee production over time.
    - **Filtering:** Filter the CPS view by building or upgrade type.
    - **Exact numbers on hover:** Tooltip overlays on graphs show precise values at any point.
    - **Lifetime stats:** Every stat is tracked across your full play history, not just the current run.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Undecided additions">
    These features are under consideration but have not been assigned to a specific version.

    - **Mobile UI:** A redesigned layout for phones and tablets, replacing the tab bar with a sidebar navigation pattern.
    - **Touch controls:** Adjusted input targets and interaction patterns optimized for touchscreens.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
